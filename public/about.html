About Rootine

Rootine began with a simple thought: keeping a habit can feel more encouraging when you can see it growing. So we made a small garden for the routines people want to make room for, with plants that change as care adds up.

A simple rhythm

How Rootine works

No grand reset needed. Just a habit, a moment of care, and another chance tomorrow.

🌰
Step 1

Plant

Choose one small habit, give it a name and pick a little symbol for your garden.

💧
Step 2

Tend

When you show up for it, tap your check-in. A missed day stays a soft dot, never a scolding.

🌼
Step 3

Grow

See the plant move from seed to bloom as your completed days collect over time.

Made for a quiet, focused experience

A little about the build

Rootine keeps the experience light: no framework on the frontend, no chart library, and a small API that stores habits and the dates you tend them.
